THE CHAMBER

Watch it decide

Seats recolour as 150 members re-decide. Senators cite their rules, ask you questions out loud and propose amendments.

A coach shows the path to passage: what you need, who blocks, who is persuadable.

Accept an amendment by voice and the bill rewrites itself.

THE PROBLEM

Big decisions get one voice

Hidden weighting

One LLM answer blends every concern into prose. You never see which part of you won, or why.

Rules are suggestions

You can tell a model “never below 7 hours of sleep”. A persuasive paragraph can still talk it out of that.

No audit trail

Ask twice, get two answers. No probabilities, no citations to your own documents, no record of what moved it.

HOW IT WORKS

From a document to a vote

1

NDI reads the constitution

Bodies, vote weights, vetoes, thresholds, override rule, each with a page citation.

2

Code forms the Senate

One senator per body plus 150 caucus members, each with a temperament and a personal stake.

3

You argue out loud

After every sentence about 180 Drex decisions: stance, cite, question, amendment, ally. Webcam via Drex Max.

4

Code enforces powers

Drex casts the votes. Weights, 2/3 rules, vetoes and override conditions are applied in code.

The LLM only drafts the menu of amendments and questions at setup, and gives senators their spoken words. It decides nothing.

ANY CONSTITUTION

Should the Senate name Caesar dictator for life?

NDI read the Roman Republic’s constitution: 11 bodies, tribunes and consuls with the veto. When the Consul argued “the tribunes could no longer block him”, the tribunes fell to 0.9 of 4.

82% voted aye. The motion still failed: the Consuls vetoed it.

Also on the shelf: your own constitution, the U.S. Constitution (National Archives), Japan.
